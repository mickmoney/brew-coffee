Our Story

Before 2020, a great cup of coffee in Ladyville meant a drive into Belize City.

Brew opened that year to give the community a coffee shop of its own. Our walk-up window was an instant hit with neighbors on their way to work, and the shop soon became a place to stay a while.

Our second home at Paradise Point serves locals and visitors staying at nearby hotels like the Radisson Fort George, with the same care in every cup.

Leaving people happier
than we found them.
